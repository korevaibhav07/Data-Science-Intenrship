# Task 13 – Customer Behavior Analysis using RFM

## Objective
The objective of this task is to analyze customer behavior and segment customers using the RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) framework to support targeted business and marketing strategies.

## Dataset Used
- sales_data.csv – transactional sales data containing order date, customer ID, and revenue details.

## Work Done
- Loaded and validated transactional sales data.
- Calculated RFM metrics for each customer:
  - Recency – days since last purchase
  - Frequency – number of purchases
  - Monetary – total revenue generated
- Assigned RFM scores (1–5) to customers.
- Segmented customers into categories such as Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk, and Potential Customers.
- Visualized customer distribution and revenue contribution by segment.
- Saved final RFM tables and segment-level outputs.

## Output Files
- rfm_table.csv – customer-level RFM metrics and scores.
- rfm_segments_revenue.csv – revenue contribution by RFM segment.
- plots/rfm_segment_count.png – customer count by RFM segment visualization.

## Conclusion
RFM analysis helps identify high-value, loyal, and at-risk customers. These insights can be used for targeted marketing, customer retention, and improving overall business performance.
