# Task 9 – Profitability & KPI Analysis

## Objective
The objective of this task is to analyze business profitability by calculating key performance indicators (KPIs) and identifying the most profitable products and customers.

## Dataset
- **sales_data.csv**
  - Transactional sales data containing order, customer, product, quantity, and pricing information.

## Work Done
- Loaded sales data and verified data quality.
- Calculated revenue for each order.
- Estimated cost and computed profit for each transaction.
- Derived key business KPIs such as total revenue, total profit, profit margin, average order value, and revenue per customer.
- Performed product-wise and customer-wise profitability analysis.
- Visualized profitability using bar charts.
- Saved all analysis outputs for reporting and reuse.

## Output Files
- **kpi_summary.csv** – Summary of key business KPIs.
- **product_profit.csv** – Revenue and profit by product.
- **customer_profit.csv** – Revenue and profit by customer.
- **plots/profit_by_product.png** – Product-wise profit visualization.
- **plots/top_customers_profit.png** – Top customers by profit visualization.

## Conclusion
This analysis provides insights into overall business performance, highlights the most profitable products and customers, and supports data-driven decision-making for improving profitability.
