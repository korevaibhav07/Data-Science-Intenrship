# Task 10 – Business Performance Dashboard

## Objective
The objective of this task is to build a consolidated business dashboard by combining sales trends, profitability analysis, and key performance indicators (KPIs) to support data-driven decision-making.

## Datasets Used
- sales_data.csv – transactional sales data
- kpi_summary.csv – overall business KPIs
- product_profit.csv – product-wise profitability
- customer_profit.csv – customer-wise profitability

## Work Done
- Loaded and validated multiple datasets from previous tasks.
- Analyzed revenue trends over time.
- Reviewed key business KPIs such as total revenue, total profit, and profit margin.
- Identified top profitable products and customers.
- Visualized business performance using charts.
- Created a dashboard-style summary combining insights from multiple analyses.
- Saved final dashboard outputs and plots.

## Outputs
- dashboard_summary.csv – summarized KPIs for dashboard view
- plots/revenue_trend.png – revenue trend visualization
- plots/profit_by_product.png – profit by product chart
- plots/top_customers_profit.png – top customers by profit chart

## Conclusion
This task presents a unified business dashboard that helps understand overall performance, profitability drivers, and revenue trends. The dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly interpret insights and make informed business decisions.
