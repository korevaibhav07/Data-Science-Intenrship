## Task 6 – Customer Segmentation

In this task, customer segmentation was performed using sales data to identify different customer groups based on purchasing behavior.

### Work Done
- Loaded sales data from previous task.
- Created customer-level features such as revenue, total orders, quantity, and average order value.
- Scaled the data and applied K-Means clustering.
- Segmented customers into low, medium, and high value groups.
- Visualized and analyzed customer segments.
- Saved final outputs in CSV format.

### Outcome

The segmentation helps identify high, medium, and low value customers, enabling better business decision-making and targeted strategies.
