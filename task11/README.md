# OM Mart Product Price Analysis

## Objective
To perform category-wise exploratory data analysis on product pricing data.

## Work Performed
- Loaded and structured product dataset.
- Checked dataset shape and data types.
- Performed descriptive statistical analysis.
- Identified missing values.
- Analyzed unique product categories.
- Computed category-wise:
  - Mean Price
  - Median Price
  - Standard Deviation
- Identified highest and lowest priced products.
- Created price segmentation using custom function.
- Generated category vs price-segment cross-tabulation.
- Applied one-hot encoding on category feature.
- Computed correlation matrix.

## Visualizations
- Bar Chart (Average Price by Category)
- Boxplot (Price Distribution by Category)
- Histogram (Overall Price Distribution)
- Correlation Heatmap

## Key Insights Generated
- Category-wise price variation
- Price distribution patterns
- Identification of extreme priced products
- Relationship between encoded category features

## Conclusion
The analysis provides structured insights into pricing behavior across categories 
and supports better understanding of product segmentation.
